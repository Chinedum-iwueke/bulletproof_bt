# ALPHA-003 BTC 5m impact-proxy reversal

The frozen claim is: **Does an extreme point-in-time BTCUSDT 5m absolute return per unit `quote_volume` predict opposite-direction close-to-close return over the next 30m?** No trade-flow or market-impact causation is claimed; the ratio is only a transient-liquidity-stress proxy.

The immutable input is the admitted Bybit BTCUSDT perpetual 1m research panel for 2023-01-01 through 2024-01-01, resampled to strict complete 5m bars. At the first 1m observation after a 5m bucket, the strategy uses that completed bucket's close-to-close return and the sum of its five point-in-time `quote_volume` observations. Missing minutes, missing `quote_volume`, non-UTC data, incomplete buckets, and signal-bar quote volume below USD 1,000,000 produce no decision. Funding, OI, mark, index, and basis are logging-only context and never gates.

The impact proxy is `abs(completed_5m_return) / completed_5m_quote_volume`. It is extreme when it meets the frozen empirical quantile of the preceding 288 or 2,016 completed eligible observations. The current observation is excluded from its own threshold. An eligible positive signal bar submits a short and an eligible negative signal bar submits a long. The engine executes on its registered next-bar clock. The research target boundary remains exactly 30 minutes after the completed signal-bar close, at which point the strategy emits a close-only exit.

The eight-variant exhaustive grid is 2 impact-proxy quantiles × 2 normalization windows × 1 both-directions setting × 2 return-shock control bands. The control band is logged for the preregistered equal-absolute-return-shock comparison; it does not alter entry selection. A fixed 20-completed-bar 5m ATR and 3× ATR stop is frozen at entry. Engine risk-at-stop sizing requests 0.5% equity risk, allows truthful clipping down to 10% utilization, caps entry and gross notional at 25% of equity and leverage at 1×, rejects unresolved stops, and forbids pyramiding and flips.

The classic event-driven engine alone owns fills, delay, 6 bps taker fees, 2 bps slippage, 1 bp spread, stops, quantity, caps, margin, accounting, and forced-liquidation detection. A doubled-cost run is mandatory. The strategy logs the raw return, quote volume, ratio, trailing threshold and percentile, direction, control band, stop, target timestamp, parameters, and decision trace. Enriched panel state may pass through standard `entry_state_*` fields; OHLCV-only input cannot compute the stated predictor and therefore safely emits no entries.

Falsify the claim if held-out opposite-signed net return is nonpositive, doubled-cost net R is nonpositive, held-out support is below 50 trades, either direction alone explains the result without preregistered support, or the extreme-ratio sample is indistinguishable from the equal-return-shock control. Expected failures are informed-flow continuation, volatility clustering, broad-market displacement, and low-volume or measurement artifacts.

This is Tier2B research evidence only. It grants no capital, live-order, promotion, or self-approval authority, and all downstream artifacts require truth certification and independent review.
